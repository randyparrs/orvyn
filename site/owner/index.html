Approve your agent's key

Your agent holds a session key. You approve it for one day, with your limits, by signing in your own wallet. Nothing is sent and nothing costs gas: the approval is a signature that the agent uses with its first payment.

1Connect your wallet

Your wallet opens the agent's account on Arc Testnet. The wallet only gives its address here; it is asked to sign in step 3.

3What you are about to sign

    The money stays in the agent's account until it pays an order into the escrow. Your wallet will ask for two signatures (payments and claims). It shows them as "Enable" with hashes, not in words: these lines are what they say, built from the same values.

    4Give the approval to the agent

    The file approvals.json was downloaded. Put it on the agent's machine (in Orvyn, aa/approvals.json). It holds your limits and your signatures, and no key. Download it again.

    Approved for one day on purpose: a new key each day, so unused days never add up. The chain enforces the limit per order, the number of orders, the end date and the gas budget.
